# Privacy policy for Pitch Care: Groundskeeper

Effective date: 2026-09-26

## Summary

Pitch Care: Groundskeeper is an offline application. It does not require an account, does not connect to a remote service, and does not include advertising or analytics.

## Information stored

The app lets a user enter a pitch profile, task completion state, weather observations, settings, and maintenance journal entries. These records are stored locally on the user's device using the operating system's application preferences storage. The app does not transmit these records to the developer or any other service.

## Information collected automatically

The app does not collect advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, camera content, microphone content, browsing history, usage analytics, crash reports, or payment information.

## Sharing

The app does not share locally entered records with third parties. The operating system may apply its own device backup, security, or diagnostic behavior; users should review their device settings for those platform features.

## Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device until the user resets local data from Settings or removes the application. Resetting local data is destructive and cannot be undone by the app.

## Children's privacy

The product is intended for groundskeeping work and is targeted at adults aged 18 and over. It does not knowingly request personal information from children.

## Changes

If the app's data behavior changes, this policy should be reviewed and updated before a release that includes the change.
